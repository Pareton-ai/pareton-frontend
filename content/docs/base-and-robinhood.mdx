## Pareton on Base and Robinhood Chain

Pareton is partnering with [ForeverMoney](https://forevermoney.ai/) to grow SN10 beyond the native Bittensor community. ForeverMoney supports the bridge, liquidity, and launch on Base and Robinhood Chain, so the Pareton team can stay focused on building the subnet.

Through this partnership, Pareton is available as an ERC-20 token on both networks, with SN10/wTAO liquidity pools on Aerodrome and Uniswap v3.
