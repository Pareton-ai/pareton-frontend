A Pareton validator sets weights on Bittensor subnet 10. It does not bench
patches and it does not rent GPUs. Benching happens once, on Pareton's
infrastructure, and the result is public.

Your job as a validator is to decide the emission split and sign it.

For how the split itself is defined, see
[Validators and emissions](/docs/platform/validators-and-emissions).

## What you are agreeing to

Every open campaign pays its seated leader a share of subnet emission. The share
follows the campaign's `emission_rule` and decays while one leader holds the
crown. Everything not paid to a leader is burned.

You recompute that split from public data and set it yourself. You do not have
to trust Pareton's published vector, and you should not have to. Every input is
readable from the [public API](/docs/platform/api) and the chain.

## Two ways to run

**Recompute (recommended).** Read the open campaigns, their emission rules, and
each seated leader from the API. Read the current block and the metagraph from
the chain. Build the vector yourself and sign it. This is the mode the system is
designed for. Decay is measured in blocks, not wall-clock time, because blocks
are the only clock you and Pareton share.

**Mirror.** Read `GET /v1/weights` and sign the vector it returns. Simpler, but
you are trusting Pareton's arithmetic instead of checking it. Use this only to
get started.

Either way, your `version_key` must match. Validators trust-weight each other
only while their keys agree.

## What you need

- A Bittensor wallet with a hotkey registered on subnet 10 (finney).
- A validator permit on that hotkey. Permits go to the top stake holders, so you
  need enough stake to hold one.
- A host that stays up. The cadence is every 360 blocks, about 72 minutes.
- Python 3.10 or later, and `bittensor` 11.x.
